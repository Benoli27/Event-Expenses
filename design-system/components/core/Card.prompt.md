Flat content block for news, sections and programme items.

```jsx
<Card tone="white" image="/assets/photography/p5-img_p4_1.jpg" eyebrow="Squirrels" title="Fun starts at four">
  Our youngest section meets on Mondays.
</Card>
```

Body copy is Light on white/subtle, Regular on a colour tone. Keep `elevated` off unless the card genuinely floats above content.
